How AI is changing the way we learn crypto.

Artificial intelligence is transforming éducation. And crypto éducation, a complex, fast-evolving field, may be where AI has the greatest impact. This guide explores how AI is used to learn cryptocurrency, what works, and what doesn't yet.

The evolution of crypto éducation

01

Articles and videos

The first generation. Thousands of articles, YouTube videos, blogs. Abundant content but no personalization. Users navigate alone.

02

Structured courses

Platforms like Coursera or Udemy offer crypto courses with defined progression. Better structured, but identical path for everyone.

03

General AI chatbots

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini answer crypto questions in real-time. Powerful for one-off questions, but no lasting memory, no path, no comprehension verification.

04

Adaptive AI mentors

The new generation. AIs designed specifically for éducation that evaluate level, build personalized paths, verify comprehension, and adapt in real-time. Caufid (Mentova) is an example.

What AI does well in crypto éducation

Personalization at scale

A human teacher can personalize for a class of 30. An AI mentor can do it for thousands of learners simultaneously, each with their own level, gaps, and pace.

Comprehension verification

An article doesn't know if you understood it. An AI mentor can ask questions, propose exercises, and verify you actually assimilated a concept before moving forward.

Current AI limitations

  • Hallucinations, AI models can generate incorrect information confidently
  • Outdated data, Models have knowledge cutoffs. Crypto evolves fast.
  • Lack of contextual judgment, AI can explain concepts but struggles to evaluate stratégies for specific financial situations

FAQ

Can AI teach crypto?
Yes. AI can explain concepts, answer questions, generate exercises, and adapt to the learner's level. Adaptive AI mentors like Caufid go further by building personalized paths and verifying real comprehension.
What's the difference between a chatbot and an AI mentor?
A chatbot answers questions on the spot. An adaptive AI mentor evaluates your level, remembers previous sessions, identifies gaps, builds a personalized path, and adapts explanations to your progress.
