Getting started with crypto: the guide for absolute beginners.

You've been hearing about Bitcoin, blockchain, and crypto for months or years. You know it matters. But you don't know where to start. This guide is for you. No unnecessary jargon. No shortcuts. Just the fundamentals, explained clearly.

Why pay attention to crypto in 2026?

Cryptocurrency is no longer a tech curiosity. In 2026, Bitcoin is recognized as a store of value by multiple countries. Stablecoins are used for daily international transfers. DeFi manages billions without banking intermediaries. Understanding crypto means understanding part of the future of finance.

But understanding doesn't mean speculating. This guide won't push you to buy anything. It gives you the tools to understand what's happening and make your own decisions.

Fundamental concepts

Blockchain

Imagine a shared ledger maintained by thousands of computers worldwide. Every transaction is written in it and nobody can erase or modify it. That's a blockchain: a transparent, decentralized, immutable digital ledger.

Bitcoin

Bitcoin is the first and most well-known cryptocurrency. Created in 2009, it lets you send value to anyone in the world without going through a bank. There will never be more than 21 million bitcoins, making it scarce by design.

Wallets

A wallet stores, receives, and sends crypto. It doesn't hold your crypto directly, it holds the private keys that prove you own them. Lose your keys, lose your crypto.

Exchanges

Exchanges are platforms where you buy, sell, and trade crypto. Binance, Coinbase, Kraken are among the best known. Keeping crypto on an exchange means trusting them with your keys.

5 steps to get started

  • Understand before investing. Read, learn, ask questions. Don't buy anything until you understand what you're doing.
  • Learn security basics. Recovery phrases, private keys, two-factor authentication. Security in crypto is your responsibility.
  • Start small. If you decide to buy, start with an amount you'd be willing to lose entirely.
  • Choose reliable sources. Avoid gurus promising returns. Prefer educational sources that explain the why, not just the what.
  • Keep learning. Crypto evolves fast. Build a habit of regular learning.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Buying without understanding, following social media tips without knowing what you're buying
  • Investing more than you can lose, crypto is volatile, the risk is real
  • Neglecting security, not backing up your recovery phrase, weak passwords
  • Chasing "the next Bitcoin", over 20,000 cryptos exist, most have no value
  • Panic selling, selling out of fear is the most common way to lose money

FAQ

What exactly is cryptocurrency?
Cryptocurrency is a digital currency that works without a central bank. It uses cryptography to secure transactions and blockchain to record them transparently and immutably.
Do I need a lot of money to start?
No. You can buy fractions of Bitcoin. Some people start with as little as $10-20. The important thing is to understand before investing.
Where should I start if I know nothing?
Start with the basics: what blockchain is, how Bitcoin works, what a wallet is. Then explore other concepts progressively. An AI mentor like Caufid can evaluate your level and create an adapted path.
