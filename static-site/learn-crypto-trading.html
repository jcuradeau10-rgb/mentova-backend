Crypto trading explained: understand before you trade.

Crypto trading attracts many. The promise of quick gains is alluring. Reality is different: the majority of traders lose money. This guide doesn't teach you to get rich. It teaches you to understand what trading actually is, its mechanics, and its risks.

Important disclaimer

Mentova is an educational platform. This guide is informational and does not constitute trading or investment advice. Crypto trading carries significant risk of capital loss.

Trading vs Investing

An investor buys an asset with a long-term view based on fundamentals. A trader seeks to profit from short to medium-term price movements. Knowing what you're doing and why is the first step.

Types of trading

Day Trading

Opening and closing positions within the same day. Requires constant chart monitoring, good risk management, and strict discipline. Extremely demanding in time and skills.

Swing Trading

Holding positions for several days to weeks, capturing larger price movements. Less time-demanding than day trading but requires solid technical analysis skills.

DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging)

Investing a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price. Not trading per se, but often the most accessible and effective strategy for beginners wanting long-term exposure.

Essential concepts

Risk Management

Probably the most important concept. Rules include:

  • Never risk more than 1-2% of your capital on a single trade
  • Always set a stop-loss before entering a position
  • Never trade with money you need
  • Accept losses as part of the process

Leverage: the beginner trap

The reality of leverage

With 10x leverage, a 10% drop liquidates your entire position. Your capital is gone. Statistics show the vast majority of leveraged traders lose their entire capital.

Most common mistakes

  • Trading without a plan — Entering positions without knowing when to exit
  • FOMO — Buying because the price is rising, without analysis
  • Revenge trading — Increasing positions after a loss to "make it back"
  • Following "signals" — Copying trades from strangers on social media

FAQ

What is crypto trading?
Crypto trading means buying and selling cryptocurrencies to profit from price variations. Unlike long-term investing, trading involves more frequent operations focused on short-term movements.
Can you make a living from crypto trading?
The vast majority of traders lose money. Studies show over 70% of retail traders end up with losses. Trading should never be considered guaranteed income.
