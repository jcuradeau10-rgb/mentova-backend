Investing in crypto: understand before you act.

Crypto investment has become accessible to everyone. But accessible doesn't mean simple. This guide doesn't tell you what to buy. It helps you understand the fundamental principles for making informed decisions, at your own pace.

Disclaimer

This guide is strictly educational. Mentova does not provide investment advice. Crypto investment carries significant risk of capital loss. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.

Fundamental principles

1. Understand before investing

The most important rule. If you don't understand what you're investing in, you won't understand why it goes up or down. And when it drops, and it always does, you'll make emotional decisions instead of rational ones.

2. Only invest what you can lose

This isn't a slogan. It's a survival rule. Crypto drops of 30-50% aren't unusual. If the invested money is needed for essentials, psychological pressure makes rational decisions impossible.

3. Think long term

History shows that investors who held positions over several years generally outperformed those who tried to time the market. Patience is probably a retail investor's greatest competitive advantage.

DCA: the most accessible strategy

DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) is simple: invest a fixed amount at regular intervals, regardless of price. It eliminates the stress of finding the "right time" to buy and smooths volatility over time.

Concrete example

If you had invested $100 per month in Bitcoin since January 2020, your total investment of $7,200 would have significantly exceeded that sum, despite the 2022 crash. DCA turns volatility into an advantage over time.

What to avoid

  • Memecoins and unknown tokens, Over 95% of altcoins lose most of their value
  • Guaranteed return promises, No guaranteed returns exist in crypto
  • Herd effect, Buying because "everyone's buying" is the definition of FOMO
  • Ignoring taxes, Crypto gains are taxable in most countries

FAQ

How much to invest when starting?
Only invest what you'd be willing to lose entirely. For beginners, starting with a small amount lets you learn without significant risk.
What is DCA?
DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) means investing a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price. It reduces volatility impact and eliminates timing stress.
