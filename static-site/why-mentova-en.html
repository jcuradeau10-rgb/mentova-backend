Why Mentova exists.

Cryptocurrency and finance are topics that affect more and more people. But for most beginners, the learning journey looks something like this:

You type "how does Bitcoin work" into Google. You find 50 million results. You read three articles. Each explains things differently. You think you understood, but when someone asks you a question, you're not sure. You don't know what to learn next. You don't know what you don't know.

Information is not what's missing. Structure, personalization and follow-up are.

The problem isn't the content

There are excellent resources for learning crypto. Educational libraries like Binance Academy offer hundreds of free articles. Online courses provide structured paths. AI chatbots can answer any question.

But none of these approaches do one simple thing: adapt to you. To your level. To your gaps. To your pace. To what you've already understood and what still escapes you.

That's why we created Caufid

Caufid is an AI mentor designed specifically for crypto and financial education. It doesn't just answer your questions. It learns to know you.

When you use Caufid for the first time, it evaluates your level. It identifies what you understand and what you're missing. It builds a learning path adapted to your profile. It offers exercises, quizzes, and real-world scenarios. It verifies that you've actually understood, not just memorized.

And when you come back the next day, it remembers. Your journey continues where you left off.

Caufid doesn't just answer your questions. It learns to know you and adapts your learning.

What Mentova is

Mentova Academy is a personalized crypto and financial education platform. It uses Caufid to create learning experiences that adapt to the level, goals and progress of each user.

The core of Mentova is free. A VIP subscription offers additional features for those who want to go further.

What Mentova is not

Mentova is not a trading platform. It doesn't buy, sell or store cryptocurrency. It doesn't provide financial advice, trading signals or investment recommendations. It doesn't promise gains.

Mentova is an educational platform. Its goal is to help you understand so you can think for yourself.

Built in Canada

Mentova is created in Canada by a team that believes financial education should be accessible, personal and honest. No false promises. No aggressive marketing. Just a tool that helps you learn.
