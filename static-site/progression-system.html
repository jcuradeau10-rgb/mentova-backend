Frequently Asked Questions

How does Mentova know my level?
Mentova uses your interactions, quiz answers, completed modules, and conversations with Caufid to progressively evaluate your level across different domains.
Does Caufid remember my progress?
Yes. Caufid has access to your progression history, skill scores, and completed modules to personalize its recommendations.
Is my path the same as everyone else?
No. Each user has a path that evolves based on their knowledge, goals, and platform usage.
Can I learn at my own pace?
Absolutely. Mentova is designed to adapt to your pace. There's no time or frequency requirement.
How do modules contribute to my progress?
Modules allow you to discover concepts, verify your understanding with quizzes, and track your mastery of each topic. Every action contributes to your XP and skill scores.
Can I go back to a concept?
Yes. You can ask Caufid at any time to re-explain a concept or create a new module on a topic you want to explore further.
Does Mentova provide financial advice?
No. Mentova is a financial education platform. Caufid is an AI educational mentor designed to help you understand financial and crypto concepts, not to provide personalized financial advice or investment recommendations.
