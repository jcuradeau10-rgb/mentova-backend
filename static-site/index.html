What exactly is a liquidity pool?
A liquidity pool is a collection of funds locked in a smart contract. Instead of a traditional order book, decentralized exchanges use these pools so people can trade directly against the available liquidity. Users who contribute funds earn a portion of the trading fees.
Let me give you a concrete example. Imagine you and a friend each put $500 into a shared pot. Anyone who wants to exchange tokens can use your pot, and you both earn a small fee every time they do.
