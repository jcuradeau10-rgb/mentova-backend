FAQ

Common questions.

What is Mentova?
Mentova Academy is a personalized crypto and financial education platform. It uses Atlas AI to create adaptive learning experiences based on each user's level, goals, and progress.
What is Atlas AI?
Atlas is an AI mentor designed specifically for crypto and financial education. It adapts to your level, remembers your progress, creates personalized modules, and tests your understanding through exercises and quizzes.
Is Atlas a financial advisor?
No. Mentova is strictly an educational platform. Atlas does not provide financial advice, trading signals, or investment recommendations. It teaches concepts so you can make more informed decisions on your own.
Is Mentova a crypto exchange?
No. Mentova does not buy, sell, store, or trade cryptocurrency. It is purely an educational platform that helps you understand the crypto and financial ecosystem.
Is Mentova suitable for beginners?
Yes. Atlas is specifically designed to adapt to any level. Complete beginners will get foundational explanations, while more experienced users will receive advanced content. Atlas adjusts based on what it learns about you.
Does Atlas remember my progress?
Yes. Atlas maintains a learning profile that includes your level, completed modules, quiz results, knowledge gaps, and learning objectives. Your journey continues from where you left off.
What languages are supported?
Mentova is available in English, French, and Spanish. The entire experience including Atlas, modules, and interface is translated.
Is Mentova free?
Core learning features are accessible for free. A VIP subscription provides additional features including unlimited Atlas access, chart analysis, daily briefings, and priority to new features.
