Mentova vs Tutor AI: specialized mentor or general AI?

General AI tutors like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini can answer almost any question, including crypto. But a specialized AI mentor like Caufid (Mentova) takes a fundamentally different approach. This guide compares both approaches objectively.

General AI tutors

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, these tools can answer questions on virtually any topic. They're powerful and versatile. But they're not specifically designed for education, let alone for a particular domain.

What Caufid does differently

  • Evaluates your level before starting, not just what you say you know, but what you actually understand
  • Identifies your gaps, concepts you don't master, even ones you don't know you don't master
  • Builds a custom path adapted to your profile, not a generic course
  • Verifies comprehension with auto-generated quizzes and exercises
  • Remembers your previous sessions and adapts accordingly
  • Tracks your progress by skill and category

When to use a general AI

  • You have a specific, one-off question
  • You're already advanced and know exactly what you're looking for
  • You need help on varied topics, not just crypto

When to use Caufid

  • You want structured, progressive learning
  • You don't know where to start
  • You want someone to identify your gaps and guide your progress
  • You want your evolution tracked over time
