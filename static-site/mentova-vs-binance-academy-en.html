Mentova vs Binance Academy: which platform to learn crypto?

Binance Academy is one of the most comprehensive free crypto education resources available. Mentova takes a fundamentally different approach with Caufid, an adaptive AI mentor. This guide compares both platforms honestly.

Overview

Binance Academy is a free educational portal created by Binance, the world's largest crypto exchange. It offers hundreds of articles, videos, and a glossary covering a wide range of crypto topics.

Mentova is built around Caufid, a personalized AI mentor that evaluates your level, identifies your gaps, builds a custom learning path, and verifies your understanding through quizzes and exercises.

When to use Binance Academy

  • You want a free, comprehensive reference on a specific crypto topic
  • You prefer reading articles or watching videos at your own pace
  • You already use the Binance exchange and want integrated education
  • You need content in a language Mentova doesn't support yet

When to use Mentova

  • You want a structured, personalized learning path
  • You don't know where to start and need guidance
  • You want someone to identify your gaps and verify your understanding
  • You want your progress tracked over time
  • You prefer an interactive conversation over reading articles

FAQ

Is Binance Academy really free?
Yes, Binance Academy is completely free. It's funded by Binance as a way to educate potential users of their exchange.
Is Mentova independent from exchanges?
Yes. Mentova is an independent educational platform. It's not affiliated with any exchange and doesn't push users toward buying or trading on any specific platform.
